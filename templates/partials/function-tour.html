Talk to Air2Growth

60-second guided tour · Pause or exit any time

You order an Air2Growth machine, and our employee delivers it, installs it on site, and shows you how to use it. The machine captures CO₂ from ambient air and supplies it to algae cultivation. With water, nutrients, and light, the algae grow. Harvesting and preparation produce liquid algae-based fertiliser.

You can check operating status, production, tank levels, and energy on the integrated touchscreen. Through IoT, Air2Growth receives machine states and fault information so our team can monitor problems. You receive service notifications and updates during maintenance. Machine monitoring collects operating states and fault codes only, with no other private information from your site.

The illustrations show a sample workflow and a time-lapse of the biological process. Ordering and service communication are separate from machine monitoring and may require contact details.
